Who We Are

Industrial Hygiene Forensics of Americus, LLC

IH-FoA is a specialized occupational and industrial hygiene consulting firm based in Marietta, Georgia. We serve manufacturing, construction, healthcare, and commercial facilities across the Southeast with rapid onsite exposure assessments and OSHA-defensible compliance support.

Our team brings 15+ years of hands-on manufacturing EHS leadership to every project — not just classroom credentials. We've worked on real factory floors, construction sites, and industrial facilities. When we identify a hazard, we know how to fix it.
